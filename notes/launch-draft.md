# Launch draft

## Main post

You press Enter. About 250ms later, Google appears—but in between, your request crosses six invisible worlds.

I built the classic “what happens when you type google.com?” interview question as an explorable 3D packet journey. You are the packet: parse the URL, chase DNS referrals, build TCP in three moves, enter a TLS tunnel, cross Google’s edge, and watch the response assemble into a page.

Easy mode explains the journey with analogies. Interview mode unlocks the real terminology, extra hotspots, source links, and a one-line answer for every concept.
